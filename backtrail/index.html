Features

What is BackTrail?

BackTrail is a free browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge that remembers the full Back history of every tab, shows it in the side panel and keeps it across browser restarts. It stores only page addresses, titles and times, in your browser; your history is never sent anywhere.

Hours of reading behind a tab no longer disappear when the browser restarts.

This tab

Where you’ve been in the current tab, newest first, with times. Click any page to go back to it; Ctrl/Cmd-click opens it in a new tab.

Survives restarts

Quit, update or crash: each tab keeps its whole trail, matched to the tab that comes back.

Recover earlier history

Right after install, tabs you already had open get their past back from your browser’s own history files, read on your computer.

Review

Tabs with more Back history than the browser keeps (shown as +N), so you can finish what’s pending before you quit.

Duplicates

Tabs open on the same address. Keep the one with the most history and close the rest.

Sites

The tabs you used since you opened the browser, for a quick pass before quitting, or every open tab by site to free memory.

Closed

Tabs you closed in the last 7 days (90 with Pro), with their history. Reopen brings the tab back with its trail.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, and your history never leaves your browser. Reads addresses and titles, never page content.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on desktop. Available on Microsoft Edge now: get it from Edge Add-ons. It also works in Google Chrome; the Chrome Web Store listing is in review.

Does BackTrail see what I read?

No. It stores the address, title and time of the pages you open in each tab, only in your browser, and never reads page content. Your history is never sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Why does my browser forget my Back history?

When Chrome restores tabs after a restart, it keeps only the last 6 Back pages of each tab. BackTrail keeps up to 500 per tab and shows them in the side panel.

What does “Recover earlier history” do?

Right after install, it rebuilds the past of tabs that were already open from your browser’s own history, and, if you choose them, its History and session files. They are read inside the extension, on your computer, and never leave it.

How long are closed tabs kept?

7 days (90 with BackTrail Pro), then they are deleted automatically. You can delete any of them earlier from the panel.

Is it free?

Yes, and it stays free: live Back history for every tab, Review, Duplicates, Sites, Closed, the full recovery scan and recovery of 3 tabs. No account and no ads.
