Privacy Policy

Current versionLast updated October 17, 2026 · ChatCairn 0.3 and later

The short version: your chats, notes and tasks stay in your browser. No account, no ChatCairn servers, no analytics, and we never sell data.

Summary

ChatCairn is a Chrome extension by LGD Ventures that saves, searches and exports your AI conversations and lets you turn highlights into notes and tasks. Everything is stored locally in your browser. LGD Ventures runs no servers that receive your conversations, notes or usage data, and ChatCairn contains no analytics, tracking or ads.

What ChatCairn reads, and when

  • The conversation you choose to save. When you click Save or Export, or save a highlight as a note/task, ChatCairn reads that one conversation from the open tab on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com or grok.com. It does not read other tabs, other sites, or chats you did not choose to save.
  • Same-site requests with your existing session (ChatGPT and Claude). To capture a conversation completely and accurately, ChatCairn may ask the site you are on for that same conversation through the site's own web endpoints (for example chatgpt.com/backend-api or claude.ai/api), using the session you are already logged in with. This happens only when you save, is read-only, and goes only to that same site. Any session token involved stays in memory for that single request and is never stored or sent anywhere else. If this fails, ChatCairn reads the visible page instead. Gemini and Grok are read from the page only.

What is stored, and where

Saved conversations, your notes and tasks (text, project, due date, done state), projects and settings are kept only in your browser (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local). If you activate Pro, your license key and activation instance id are stored locally too. Uninstalling the extension deletes this data, so make a backup first.

Network requests ChatCairn makes

  • The AI site you are on — only as described above, when you save.
  • Lemon Squeezy License API (api.lemonsqueezy.com) — only if you use Pro: to activate, validate (about once a week) and deactivate your license. It sends your license key and an instance id; no chat content.
  • Google Drive (optional, currently not available) — a planned Pro auto-backup will, only if you turn it on, upload backup files to a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata, which cannot see your other Drive files). Google access tokens are kept in session memory only.

No other requests are made. Checkout happens on Lemon Squeezy's website, which acts as merchant of record under its own privacy policy.

Backups and problem reports

Manual backups are JSON files you download and keep yourself. “Report a problem” opens your own email app with technical counters (selector counts, version, browser) and no chat content; nothing is sent unless you send that email to lgdventures.hq@gmail.com. “Send feedback” in Settings works the same way: it opens an email with only the ChatCairn version and your browser name and version filled in.

Uninstall survey

When you remove ChatCairn, Chrome opens a short optional survey page on this site (lgdventures.github.io/chatcairn/uninstall/). The only information in its address is the ChatCairn version number, with no identifiers, chat data or usage data. The page has no analytics or tracking. If you choose to answer, it opens your own email app with the reasons you picked and your comment; nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Export footer and share prompt

Markdown, PDF and ZIP exports end with one line, “Made with ChatCairn”, and a link to this site (tagged utm_source=export_footer). It is plain text inside the file you export; ChatCairn adds nothing else and sends nothing. On the Free plan it is always there; Pro can turn it off in Settings. A few times at most (after your 10th saved chat, your first export and your first note from a highlight) ChatCairn may suggest sharing it. That prompt only copies a link to this site, or opens X, Reddit or your email app with a suggested message for you to post or send yourself. To limit how often it appears, ChatCairn keeps a few local counters in your browser (which prompts were shown, and whether you chose “Don’t show again”). They never leave your device, and ChatCairn never reads your contacts.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose other than ChatCairn's single purpose: saving, searching and exporting your AI conversations and the notes/tasks you create from them.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs and from the websites you use ChatCairn on adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. If Google Drive backup is enabled in the future, ChatCairn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

Delete any chat or note inside ChatCairn at any time; deactivate Pro from the Pro dialog; remove all local data by uninstalling the extension. Because we don't hold your data, there is nothing on our side to request or delete — but you can always contact us.

Changes and contact

We will update this page (and its date) if ChatCairn's data practices change. Questions: lgdventures.hq@gmail.com · LGD Ventures.
