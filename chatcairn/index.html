FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where are my chats stored?

Only in your browser (IndexedDB and Chrome storage). ChatCairn has no servers that receive your conversations. See the privacy policy.

How does ChatCairn read a conversation?

When you click Save, it reads that one chat from the page. On ChatGPT and Claude it may ask the site itself for the same conversation using your existing login, read-only. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Monthly or lifetime?

Both unlock the same Pro features. Monthly is $4.99 and can be cancelled anytime; lifetime is a one-time $39. One key activates up to 3 browsers.

Where do I find my license key?

In your Lemon Squeezy receipt email (subject “Your ChatCairn Pro … receipt”) and in My Orders at app.lemonsqueezy.com. Paste it in ChatCairn → Activate license.

What happens at the free limit?

Everything you saved stays available to read, search and export. Upgrade to keep saving new chats and notes.
