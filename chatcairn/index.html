Features

What is ChatCairn?

ChatCairn is a browser extension for Chrome and Edge, for people who use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok. It saves chats into one local, searchable library, exports them to Markdown or PDF, and turns highlights into notes and tasks. Your chats stay in your browser: no account and no ChatCairn server.

Notes & tasks from highlights New in 0.3

Select any passage right in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok and keep it as a note or a task with a project, due date and comment, without saving the whole conversation. It works in saved chats too. Filter by Overdue, Today or Upcoming, and jump back to the source in one click. Tasks export in Obsidian Tasks syntax.

One-click save

Save the open conversation from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok: your prompts and the answers, with code, tables and headings intact.

Search everything

Full-text search across every chat you saved, on every platform, plus a home view of what you saved lately.

Markdown, PDF & ZIP

Markdown with front-matter for Obsidian and Notion, clean paginated PDFs, or a ZIP of your whole library.

Private, with backups

Everything stays in your browser: no account, no ChatCairn servers, no analytics. One-click backup and merge-restore keep it safe. GPT Rescue also saves the Custom GPTs and Gems you created, free: How to export your Custom GPTs and Gems.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which browsers does it work in?

Desktop Google Chrome (Chrome Web Store) and Microsoft Edge (Edge Add-ons). Mobile browsers don't support extensions like this.

Why does Chrome say “not trusted by Enhanced Safe Browsing”?

With Enhanced protection on, Chrome shows “Proceed with caution” for extensions from developers it doesn’t know yet. Google says new developers generally take a few months to become trusted. ChatCairn is reviewed and published on the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons, and your chats stay on your device, so it’s safe to choose Continue to install.

Where are my chats stored?

Only in your browser (IndexedDB and extension storage). ChatCairn has no servers that receive your conversations. See the privacy policy.

How does ChatCairn read a conversation?

When you click Save, it reads that one chat from the page. On ChatGPT and Claude it may ask the site itself for the same conversation using your existing login, read-only. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
