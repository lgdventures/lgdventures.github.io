How to export a Gemini or Grok chat to PDF

GuidePublished Nov 9, 2026 · Last checked Nov 9, 2026

How do you export a Gemini or Grok chat to PDF?

Quick answer: Print the open chat for a rough PDF. For one Gemini response, Google documents an export to a Doc, which you can then download. For Grok, the consumer FAQ documents a public share link and an account data download, not a one-chat PDF. To keep a PDF of the chat you choose, save it in ChatCairn and export from the local library. Free covers 30 saved chats. Pro is $4.99/month or $69 lifetime.

Export a Gemini chat to PDF

Method 1: Print the open page

This uses the browser. It does not depend on a Gemini menu.

  1. Open the conversation on gemini.google.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Ctrl+P (Windows or Linux) or Cmd+P (Mac).
  3. Choose Save as PDF and save.

What to expect: the file is the page the browser lays out. It can include the site sidebar and the browser’s own header or footer. Messages that the page has not laid out are not in the file. It is a snapshot, not an archive Google documents as a full export.

Method 2: One Gemini response, through Google Docs

Per Google Help: Export responses from Gemini Apps, you must be signed in. Export options can differ on a Google Workspace account, and they vary by Gemini app. For a response:

  1. Below the response, click Share & export, then Export to Docs.
  2. Google says this saves a new Doc in the Google account’s Drive. That is Google’s Doc, not a ChatCairn backup.
  3. Open the Doc. Google Docs Help says to click File, then Download, and choose a file type. For a large Doc in Chrome, the same page says File, then Print, set the destination to Save as PDF, and click Save.

That help page documents one response. It does not document a button that writes the whole conversation to a formatted PDF. The same article also documents export to Gmail, Sheets for a table (tables with images cannot be exported to Sheets), Colab and Replit. Those are not PDF exports of the chat.

Method 3: Google Takeout, if you want the account archive

Per Google Help: Download your Gemini Apps data, Takeout can include Gemini Apps activity such as chats, generated media and uploads. Downloading does not delete that activity from Google. The result is an archive, not a clean one-chat PDF.

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the same Google account you use for Gemini Apps.
  2. Under “Select data to include,” choose Deselect all.
  3. For Gems, check Gemini. For chats, check My Activity, open All activity data included, choose Deselect all in that dialog, check Gemini Apps, then Ok.
  4. Choose a delivery method. If you pick an email download link, Google says the link is active for 7 days. You can also add the archive to Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Box.
  5. Choose export once, or a recurring export where that option is offered, then zip or tgz, and create the export.

Google says it can take a few hours to a few days, and that most people get the link the same day. Changes made while the archive is being created may be missing.

Export a Grok chat to PDF

Method 1: Print the open page

  1. Open the conversation on grok.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Ctrl+P or Cmd+P.
  3. Choose Save as PDF.

Same limit as Gemini: you get the laid-out page, which can include the site chrome. This guide is for grok.com. xAI’s consumer FAQ says Grok inside the X product uses that product’s own help center.

Method 2: What xAI’s consumer FAQ documents

Per xAI’s consumer FAQ, checked 9 November 2026:

  • Share on grok.com, from the top right or the share icon under the conversation, creates a public link. It is not a file. Anyone with the link can open it, and a link posted in public can be indexed by a search engine. You can remove a link at grok.com/share-links.
  • Download your data from the Grok app or grok.com under Settings and Data Controls, or send a request at x.ai/privacy-portal. The FAQ says you can access, download and delete your data there. It does not say that download is a PDF of one conversation, and it does not name the file format.

We are not describing an “export this message to PDF” button, because that FAQ does not list one. If the product UI shows a control the FAQ does not mention, follow the label on screen and treat this page as the documented path only.

FAQ

Can I export a whole Gemini chat to PDF without an extension?

Print the open page. Google’s export help documents one response sent to a Doc, not a formatted PDF of the whole conversation. Takeout downloads Gemini Apps activity as an archive.

Does Grok’s consumer FAQ include a one-chat PDF?

No. The page we checked documents a public share link and a data download from Settings and Data Controls. It does not call that download a PDF of one conversation.

Does ChatCairn store the chat in the cloud?

No. The copy you save stays in this browser. There is no ChatCairn account and no automatic or cloud backup. Export JSON, Markdown or PDF when you want a file on your device.
