ChatCairn Privacy Policy

  • _Last updated: October 16, 2026_
  • ChatCairn runs entirely in your browser.
  • **What it reads:** the text of the AI conversation open in your tab on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com or grok.com, only when you click Save or Export.
  • **Where it is stored:** locally in your browser (IndexedDB and chrome.storage). We have no servers and never receive your conversations.
  • **Network:** the only external request is to api.lemonsqueezy.com to activate/validate a Pro license key (sends the key and an instance id). No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling of data.
  • **Deletion:** delete any conversation in the library, or uninstall the extension to remove all data.
  • **Payments** are processed by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record) under its own privacy policy.
  • Contact: chatcairn.app@gmail.com (placeholder)
