Products

What does LGD Ventures make?

Two browser extensions, each built for one job, and both keep your data on your device. ChatCairn is a Chrome extension for people who use AI chatbots that saves, searches and exports ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats. MeetCrate is a Chrome and Edge extension for Read AI users that exports every meeting transcript at once.

Live · Chrome & Edge

ChatCairnChrome extension

Save, search and export your ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok chats in one library, then turn what matters into notes and tasks. Export to Markdown or PDF. Everything stays on your device.

  • ChatGPT
  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Grok
  • Notes & tasks
  • Markdown & PDF
  • 100% local
